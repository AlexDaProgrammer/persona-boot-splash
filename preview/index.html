Boot time impact

Your boot itself is unchanged. The splash starts after you sign in, takes about 0.3 to 1 second to open, then runs for 3 seconds. Any key or click skips straight to the exit.

The Dark Hour

Dark Hour opens with the midnight clock: it ticks over, turns green and shatters, adding 3 seconds. On your PC it switches on by itself between midnight and 1am. Persona 5 mode is manual: pick it with the mode switcher. Use the buttons to see both here.

On your PC

The blades open onto your real desktop, and the readout shows your real PC name, uptime, battery, Windows power mode and free disk space. Here they're example values.
